About

Asi Cohen is an Israeli actor, comedian, and screenwriter based in Givatayim. He is available for television, film, stage, and commercial work.

He is best known as Shauli in HaParlament and Eretz Nehederet, and as Chai Tortel in HaTortelim. Casting and production teams also know him from Mesudarim, Kacha Ze, the drama Hatufim, and films such as Colombian Love and This Is Sodom.

Beyond acting, he writes and develops comedy - including writing and editing on HaParlament - and performs stand-up in character. He has won six Israeli Television Academy Awards, with a range that covers satire, drama, and lead comedy roles.

Selected projects

  • HaParlament

    The Eretz Nehederet spin-off (2012–2014). He wrote, acted, and edited, playing Shauli - a role that earned him the Israeli Television Academy Award.

  • HaTortelim

    Sketches and a VOD series from Eretz Nehederet. He plays Chai Tortel, opposite Alma Zak as Sapir, in the nouveau riche family that took over the screen.

  • Eretz Nehederet

    A regular on Israel’s main satire show. Among his characters and impressions: Shauli, Avigdor Lieberman, Uri Geller, Gabi Ashkenazi, and more.
